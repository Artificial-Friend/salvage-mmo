# Roadmap

Milestones match GitHub milestones; issues hold the detail. The agreed gameplay steps, in build order, are in [`GAMEPLAY.md`](GAMEPLAY.md).

## M1 · Foundation
Repository, CI, tests, standalone hosting, run telemetry for balance, split the client into real modules.

## M2 · Multiplayer in one zone
Authoritative server running the native core; 2–8 players sharing Halvard's Wake; other players as IR contacts; lost player ships become persistent wrecks with their fittings attached; Newtonian netcode (prediction, interpolation, tethers between clients).

## M3 · Combat with consequences
Component damage (radiators, engines, sensors), harpooning parts off enemy ships mid-fight, PvP rules by zone risk tier.

## M4 · Living world
Non-regenerating wreck supply fed by AI faction battles, aging intel reports, a system jump map, zone access by distance and fuel.

## M5 · Depth and polish
Research tree, faction mount adapters (mismatched parts cost efficiency), modular art kit, GTAO/SSR on the WebGPU path, sound pass.
